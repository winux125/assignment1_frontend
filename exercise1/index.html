Born 1984 · founder of VKontakte and Telegram

About Pavel Durov

Pavel Valeryevich Durov was born in Leningrad in 1984 and grew up partly in Italy before studying philology at Saint Petersburg State University. In 2006 he and his brother Nikolai launched VKontakte, which became the largest social network in the Russian speaking world. He left both the company and the country in 2014 after refusing a government request for the data of Ukrainian protest organisers. The year before that he had already started Telegram, a messenger built around Nikolai's MTProto protocol, which he has funded largely out of his own pocket in order to keep it free of advertising pressure.

Timeline

  1. 1984 Born on 10 October in Leningrad, then part of the Soviet Union.
  2. 2006 Launches VKontakte with his brother Nikolai while still a student.
  3. 2013 Releases the first version of Telegram, built on MTProto.
  4. 2014 Refuses to hand over user data, is removed as VK chief executive and leaves Russia.
  5. 2018 Telegram is blocked in Russia and keeps working through proxies for two years until the ban is lifted.
  6. 2024 Detained in France over moderation on the platform and released under judicial supervision, while Telegram passes 900 million monthly users.

Contributions

  • VKontakte, the social network that defined the Russian speaking web for a decade
  • Telegram, a cross platform messenger with no advertising driven feed
  • Backing MTProto, the custom transport protocol Telegram runs on
  • The Telegram Bot API, which turned a chat app into an application platform
  • Channels and large public groups as a publishing format rather than a chat feature
  • Long running public argument that end to end encryption should be a default, not a feature
