Project description

Our midterm project is a website for Mousse, a small patisserie. The site gives the shop a proper catalogue of its cakes, cupcakes and pastries, and it lets a visitor request a custom cake for a specific date instead of having to phone the counter. The people we are designing for are dessert lovers browsing for something to buy today, and people organising a birthday, a wedding or another celebration who need a cake made to order. The purpose is to move both of those jobs online, so the shop spends less time answering the same questions and the customer can see prices and options before getting in touch. All five pages share one header, navigation bar and footer so the site reads as a single place rather than five separate documents.

Group project. Members: Baubek Serikbay, Maussymzhan Makazhan.

Sitemap

Five pages, all reachable from the navigation menu on every page, so the structure is one level deep and nothing is buried.

  • Mousse
    • Home
    • Catalog
    • Custom cakes
    • About us
    • Contact

The Home hero links to Catalog, and both Home and Catalog link to Custom cakes, but every page is also one click from every other page through the menu.
